caring for your flurr piece

flurr plushies are made from soft chenille and cotton yarns, and the flowers from cotton too, not plastic, and never kept in a vase. A little care keeps them looking just-made.

  • Keep out of direct sun — bright, undyed colours can fade with constant light.
  • Spot clean only. A damp cloth and a little mild soap lift most marks without matting the chenille.
  • If a flower needs a refresh, a gentle cool-water rinse and an air-dry is safest.
  • Handle gently by the body, not the limbs or stems — hand-tied knots hold up best with normal, loving use.
